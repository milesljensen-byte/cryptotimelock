The main ways to lock crypto

Some methods only add friction. Only one makes selling genuinely impossible until a date you choose.

Why a time-lock can't be broken

You deposit your crypto, pick an unlock date, and confirm. From that moment the funds are held by code that contains no function to release them early. No override, no emergency withdraw, no support line to call.

It stays self-custody throughout. Only your wallet can pull the funds, and only once the timer hits zero. When your date arrives, you withdraw. Not before.

Questions, answered

How do I lock my crypto so I can't sell it?

Deposit it into a time-lock vault: a smart contract where you set an unlock date. Until that date passes there's no way to withdraw, so panic selling is off the table.

Can I unlock my crypto early?

No. The contract has no function to withdraw before your unlock date. Not you, not the developer, not anyone. That's the entire point.

Is time-locking my crypto safe?

It's self-custody. Your crypto sits in a public contract on Ethereum, not with a company. Only your wallet can withdraw it, and only after the unlock date.

How long can I lock my crypto for?

Anywhere from a few weeks to ten years. You set the date when you create the lock, and it can't be shortened afterwards.

What does it cost?

A one-time 0.5% fee when you lock. Withdrawing is free. You also pay standard Ethereum gas per transaction.
