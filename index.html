Under the hood

How the lock actually works.

Same system, three depths. Drag the dial to the level that suits you.

It's a safe with a timer

When you lock crypto here, it goes into a digital safe on the Ethereum network. You choose the date the door opens. Until that date, the door will not open for anyone.

Nobody has a spare key

The safe is run by a computer program whose rules were permanently fixed the day it was created. We can't open it early. You can't either. There is no override and no support line that can help.

This website is just the remote

Your money never sits with us or on this site. If the website vanished tomorrow, your safe would still be on the network, still counting down, and still opening on your date.

Getting it back

When your date arrives, you press withdraw and the safe hands the crypto back to the exact same wallet that locked it. Withdrawing costs nothing.

An immutable contract holds your funds

Deposits go to a verified, non-upgradeable smart contract on Ethereum mainnet, not to a company wallet. Each lock records your address, the token, the amount, and your unlock timestamp on-chain.

Withdrawal has exactly three checks

You must be the wallet that created the lock, the lock must not already be withdrawn, and the block time must be past your unlock time. That's the whole gate. No early-withdraw function exists in the code.

No admin power over your funds

No admin key can touch locked principal. The emergency pause only stops new deposits; withdrawals can never be paused. The 0.5% fee is taken once at deposit, and withdrawing is free.

The frontend is optional

The contract source is verified on Etherscan. You can read every line, view your locks, and withdraw by calling the contract directly, with this site online or not.

See for yourself: verified contract on Etherscan ↗

Three requires, nothing else

withdraw(uint256) checks lock.owner == msg.sender, !lock.withdrawn, and block.timestamp >= unlockTime. State is written before transfer, guarded by nonReentrant. No other selector moves principal.

No proxy, no upgrade path

A single plain contract: no delegatecall, no upgradeability, immutable bytecode. Bare receive() reverts, so ETH only enters via lockNative(); ERC-20s via lockToken() against an allowlist.

Constrained admin surface

The owner is a multisig, and owner ops (allowlist, role rotations) sit behind 48-hour timelocks. pause() gates deposits only, auto-expires after 30 days, and anyone may unpause after that. Withdrawals are never pausable.

Verify, don't trust

Source is verified on Etherscan at 0x571B…794c. Diff the deployed bytecode, read the ABI, and confirm yourself that no early-exit path exists. That beats anything this page can claim.

Terms of Use

How it works

When you lock funds, they are sent directly to a smart contract and not to us. We never hold, control, or have access to your funds at any point. Only your wallet can withdraw them after the lock period ends.

This website is just an interface

This site is a simple front-end that helps your wallet talk to the TimeLock smart contract on Ethereum. The contract lives on the blockchain on its own — so even if this website ever goes offline, your locked funds remain safe and can still be withdrawn by interacting with the contract directly. We don't run the blockchain, your wallet, or your internet connection.

Who can use TimeLock

You should be old enough to enter a legal agreement where you live (usually 18) and able to use a tool like this under your local laws. Please only use TimeLock if that's the case for you.

Your responsibility

By using TimeLock you confirm that:

  • You understand how crypto wallets and smart contracts work.
  • You are locking funds you can afford to have inaccessible for the chosen period.
  • You are responsible for keeping your wallet and private keys safe.
  • You are not on any sanctions list and won't use TimeLock for anything illegal, such as money laundering or funding crime.

Fees

A 0.5% fee is charged at the time of locking. This fee is non-refundable.

No early release

Once funds are locked, they cannot be released before the chosen unlock time. Not even by us.

Token blacklisting risk

Some tokens including USDT, USDC, and PYUSD are issued by centralised companies that can blacklist individual wallet addresses. If your address is blacklisted by a token issuer after you have locked funds, withdrawing those specific funds may become impossible. This is an external risk outside of TimeLock's control. If you are locking a centralised token, make sure you understand this risk.

Lost wallet

If you lose access to the wallet that created a lock, the funds in that lock are permanently inaccessible. TimeLock has no admin override or recovery mechanism. Only the original locking wallet can withdraw. Keep your private keys and seed phrase safe.

Not financial or tax advice

TimeLock is a tool, not advice. Nothing on this site is financial, legal, investment, or tax advice. If you're unsure whether locking your crypto is right for you, it's a good idea to speak with a qualified professional first.

Taxes are up to you

Locking, unlocking, or holding crypto can have tax consequences depending on where you live. Working out and paying any taxes you owe is your responsibility, not ours.

No guarantees

No audit is a guarantee of security and vulnerabilities may still exist that were not detected. No company, developer, or auditor accepts liability for any loss of funds. Smart contracts are experimental technology. Use at your own risk and never lock more than you can afford to lose.

Changes

We may update this website or these terms from time to time, and the most recent version will always be the one shown here. The smart contract itself can't be changed once it's deployed — it will always behave exactly the way it was written.
